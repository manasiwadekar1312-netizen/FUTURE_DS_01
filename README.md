# Business Sales Performance Analytics  
### Data Science & Analytics – Task 1

## Project Overview
This project analyzes business sales data to understand overall performance and identify key revenue drivers. Simple and clear data visualizations created in Excel are used to answer important business questions and support data-driven decision making.

## Dataset
**Superstore Sales Dataset (CSV format)**  
The dataset contains sales-related information such as order date, product details, category, region, quantity, and sales value.  
The raw dataset is stored in the **Data** folder.

## Dashboard and Chart Explanation

### Monthly Sales Trend (2014–2017)
This chart shows how total sales change month by month over several years.  
It highlights overall growth in sales and reveals seasonal patterns, helping businesses plan inventory and marketing activities during high-demand periods.

### Sales Performance by Category
This chart compares total sales across different product categories.  
Some categories perform better than others and contribute more to revenue.  
This insight helps businesses focus on profitable categories and improve weaker ones.

### Top 10 Products by Sales
This chart displays the top ten products based on sales value.  
A small number of products generate a large portion of total revenue.  
These products are important for business growth and should be prioritized.

## Key Business Insights
- Sales show an overall increasing trend over time, indicating positive business growth.  
- Certain product categories contribute more significantly to revenue.  
- A limited number of top products generate a major share of total sales.  
- Category-level and product-level analysis helps identify strong and weak segments.

## Tools and Technologies

- Pivot Tables

  ## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how sales data can be transformed into meaningful insights using simple analytics techniques.
